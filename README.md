# Udhyam-Udaan

Udhyam-Udaan is a project developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) with the aim of helping aspiring and existing rural entrepreneurs understand, plan, and grow their businesses more easily.

The idea behind the project is simple — a lot of people have the skills and motivation to start a small business, but finding the right information, resources, schemes, and guidance can be difficult.

Udhyam-Udaan brings these things together in one place and tries to make the process simpler and more accessible.

## What does it do?

The platform is designed to help users:

- Understand different opportunities for starting a business
- Find relevant government schemes and support
- Get guidance based on their business requirements
- Explore useful resources for entrepreneurship
- Make better decisions while planning their business

## Why did we build it?

While working on the SIH problem statement, we noticed that information related to entrepreneurship is often scattered across different platforms.

Someone who wants to start a small business may have to search through multiple websites to find information about schemes, funding, training, and other support.

We wanted to build something that makes this process easier and more approachable.
